# Task 4 – Data Visualization and Storytelling
## Project Report

### 1. Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze the Sample Superstore dataset using Power BI and transform the analysis into a clear business story through interactive visualizations.

The analysis focuses on sales, profit, regional performance, category performance, sub-category profitability, and discount-related profitability patterns.

---

### 2. Dataset

The analysis uses the Sample Superstore dataset containing order, sales, profit, quantity, discount, region, category, and sub-category information.

The cleaned dataset was used for visualization and analysis.

---

### 3. Tool Used

**Power BI Desktop**

Power BI was used to create the dashboard, calculate profit margin, analyze trends, and communicate business insights.

---

### 4. Dashboard Structure

The dashboard contains five pages.

#### Page 1 – Executive Overview

Key performance indicators were created for:

- Total Sales: 2.33M
- Total Profit: 292.30K
- Total Quantity Sold: approximately 39K

A monthly sales trend was also created to identify sales patterns over time.

**Key takeaway:** Sales show strong seasonal variation, with the highest performance occurring in November and December.

---

#### Page 2 – Regional Performance

The regional analysis includes:

- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Profit Margin by Region

The West region performs strongly in both sales and profit.

The Central region records the lowest profit margin, at approximately 8%, while the West region has the highest margin at approximately 15%.

**Key takeaway:** The West region is the strongest performer, while the Central region requires profitability improvement.

---

#### Page 3 – Category & Sub-Category Analysis

The category analysis compares:

- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Sub-Category

Technology generates the highest sales and profit among the major categories.

Furniture generates substantial sales but comparatively lower profit.

Several sub-categories contribute strongly to profit, while some sub-categories generate negative profit.

**Key takeaway:** Technology is the strongest category, while Furniture and selected sub-categories require closer profitability analysis.

---

#### Page 4 – Profitability Problem Analysis

Two analytical visuals were created:

1. Sales vs Profit by Sub-Category
2. Average Discount vs Profit

The Sales vs Profit analysis identifies sub-categories where high sales do not necessarily translate into high profit.

Tables is a notable example of relatively high sales combined with negative profit.

The discount analysis shows that some sub-categories with relatively higher average discounts also have weak or negative profitability.

**Key takeaway:** Certain products generate sales without generating sufficient profit, highlighting opportunities to review pricing and discount practices.

> Note: The relationship between discount and profit is treated as an observed association, not proof that higher discounts directly cause lower profit.

---

### 5. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. **Improve Central-region profitability**  
   Review pricing, discounting, product mix, and operating costs in the Central region.

2. **Protect Technology performance**  
   Continue investing in high-performing Technology products while monitoring profit margins.

3. **Review Furniture profitability**  
   Analyze Furniture pricing, discount levels, and product-level costs.

4. **Control excessive discounting**  
   Establish discount thresholds and monitor low-margin or loss-making sub-categories.

5. **Replicate successful regional practices**  
   Study successful practices in the West region and evaluate whether they can be applied to other regions.

---

### 6. Priority Action

The highest priority should be improving profitability in the Central region and low-margin sub-categories while maintaining growth in high-performing Technology products.

---

### 7. Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard converts the Superstore dataset into a business-focused analytical story.

The analysis demonstrates that strong sales do not always translate into strong profitability. Regional performance, product category, sub-category profitability, and discount patterns should therefore be considered together when making business decisions.

The dashboard provides a foundation for management to identify areas requiring attention and prioritize profitability improvement.
