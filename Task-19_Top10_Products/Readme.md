# Task 19 – Top 10 Products by Sales

## Objective
Identify the Top 10 products based on total sales from the Superstore dataset.

## Business Question
Which products generate the highest total sales?

## Methodology
1. Grouped records by `Product Name`.
2. Aggregated `Sales` using Sum.
3. Sorted products in descending order of total sales.
4. Selected the Top 10 products.
5. Checked the Top-10 cutoff against Rank 11 for ties.
6. Created a horizontal bar chart.
7. Calculated Top-10 combined sales and contribution to total sales.
